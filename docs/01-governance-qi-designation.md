# Governance & Qualified Individual Designation — 16 CFR §314.4(a)

[← Back to README](../README.md)

## Requirement

The GLBA Safeguards Rule requires a covered institution to designate a single **Qualified Individual (QI)** responsible for overseeing, implementing, and enforcing its Information Security Program. The rule doesn't require a specific title or department — it requires clear, written accountability.

## Approach

The institution's ownership issued a short written designation memo that:

- Names the QI by role and formally assigns them responsibility for the Information Security Program
- Enumerates the QI's authority across every other 314.4 element (risk assessment, safeguards, testing, training, service provider oversight, program evaluation, incident response, and board reporting)
- Establishes a direct reporting line from the QI to ownership, including a minimum annual written report

Keeping this as a one-page, signed document (rather than folding it into a longer policy) makes it easy to produce on request during an accreditation or Department of Education review — it's the first artifact most reviewers ask for.

## Why this matters

Every other requirement in 314.4 assumes someone is unambiguously *on the hook* for it. Auditors and accreditors look for this designation first because it establishes who they can hold accountable for everything that follows — and it's the anchor citation for GV.RR (Roles, Responsibilities, and Authorities) in the NIST CSF 2.0 crosswalk below.
